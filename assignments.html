Master of Science (M.Sc.) Semester: 4051 Homeworks & Project

Assignments & Project Work

Structured hands-on assignments (HW1–HW3) with 2-week completion windows, followed by a capstone graduate research project.

Assignment 1 Weight: 10% Upcoming

Word Representations, Autoregressive Language Models & Subword Tokenization

Scheduled Release: Week 3 Deadline: Week 5 (2 Weeks Duration)

Hands-on implementation covering continuous word representations, autoregressive neural language modeling, and subword tokenization algorithms. Detailed specifications and starter repository will be released by the teaching team.

Expected Deliverables & Scope:

  • Implementation codebase and evaluation scripts.
  • Experimental comparison report and theoretical analysis.
Specifications Released in Week 3
Assignment 2 Weight: 10% Upcoming

Transformers & Parameter-Efficient Fine-Tuning (PEFT / LoRA)

Scheduled Release: Week 6 Deadline: Week 8 (2 Weeks Duration)

Hands-on implementation of Transformer multi-head attention and parameter-efficient fine-tuning (LoRA / QLoRA) on open-weight language models. Detailed specifications will be released by the teaching team.

Expected Deliverables & Scope:

  • PyTorch Transformer modules and LoRA adapter code.
  • Fine-tuning logs, checkpoint weights, and evaluation report.
Specifications Released in Week 6
Assignment 3 Weight: 10% Upcoming

Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG), Reasoning (CoT) & Model Alignment (DPO)

Scheduled Release: Week 10 Deadline: Week 12 (2 Weeks Duration)

Hands-on implementation of dense/hybrid retrieval-augmented generation (RAG), Chain-of-Thought reasoning verification, and preference alignment via Direct Preference Optimization (DPO). Detailed specifications will be released by the teaching team.

Expected Deliverables & Scope:

  • RAG pipeline implementation and DPO alignment code.
  • Evaluation results on benchmark QA datasets and technical report.
Specifications Released in Week 10
Assignment 4 (Course Project) Weight: 20% Upcoming

Capstone Research & Applied LLM Systems Project

Scheduled Release: Week 4 Deadline: Week 15 (Presentations: POST1 & POST2)

Open-ended graduate Master's research project in small teams (1–3 members). Topics encompass novel alignment methods, specialized reasoning agents, multimodal modeling, model compression, or Persian/Multilingual NLP benchmarks. Guidelines and milestones will be announced by the instructor.

Expected Deliverables & Scope:

  • Project Proposal & Topic Selection (Week 6).
  • Midterm Milestone Progress Checkpoint (Week 11).
  • Final Codebase, Model Artifacts, and IEEE/ACL format Research Report.
  • Oral Presentation & Poster Defense in Final Sessions (POST1 & POST2 in Week 15).
Specifications Released in Week 4 Project Details Page
