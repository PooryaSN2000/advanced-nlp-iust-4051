Hands-On Capstone Official Release: Monday, 1405-07-07

Project Overview & Preparation Guide

Welcome to the course capstone project! The teaching team is putting the finishing touches on your project starter kit—including beginner-friendly walkthrough notebooks, preprocessed dataset splits, and clean baseline scaffolding. Our goal is to ensure a smooth, rewarding, and highly practical learning experience that equips you with production-grade skills in modern NLP and RAG systems without feeling overwhelmed.

Paced & Guided
45% Total • 5 Milestones

Distributed across the semester (2–3 weeks per milestone). Each milestone builds naturally on previous steps, supported by friendly 1-on-1 TA check-ins.

Practical Domain
Iranian Legal Corpus

Build an intelligent assistant answering questions on Iranian Civil and Family statutory law—a practical, high-value AI application tailored for Persian NLP.

Personal Portfolio
Individual Project & Community

Develop your personal portfolio repository with provided templates. You are warmly encouraged to brainstorm ideas and discuss concepts with peers!
